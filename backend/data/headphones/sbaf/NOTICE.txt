The .txt files are copied unchanged. The measurements are 1/6-octave smoothed and normalised to 500-1500 Hz, and they are already
compensated with the SBAF curve (a straight line is "perceptually neutral"), which is not the Harman curve. They are used in PySQA
as the frequency response of the headphone in the Audio Preparation page (Headphone block). The two channels (L and R) are averaged.
